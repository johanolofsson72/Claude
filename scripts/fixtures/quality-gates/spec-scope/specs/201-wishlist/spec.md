# Spec

Customers keep a wishlist.

## Scope

In scope: adding and removing products on a logged-in customer's wishlist.

Out of scope: sharing, notifications, price alerts.

## Acceptance criteria

- A logged-in customer adds a product; it appears on /wishlist.
- Removing a product takes it off the list.
- When a wishlisted product drops in price, the customer receives an email within 1 hour.

## Notes

The register row carries the one-line goal; this file is the source of truth for the behaviour.
Any change to the criteria goes through clarify before plan.
